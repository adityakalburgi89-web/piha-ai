Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about how ElevateVoice works with your sales team.

How do I add or change the voice audio files? +
Simply place your audio files in the public/audio/ folder named english.mp3, hindi.mp3, and kannada.mp3. The player will automatically load and play them.
Can customers interrupt the assistant at any time? +
Yes! Just like talking to a human, if a customer speaks up or asks a quick question, the assistant stops speaking immediately and listens to them.
How does the instant WhatsApp follow-up work? +
While on the call, if the customer asks for a portfolio or shows high interest, the system automatically sends a personalized WhatsApp message with your project links and brochures.
