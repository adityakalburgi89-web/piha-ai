# Voice Audio Files Directory

Place your custom recorded or synthesized MP3 voice files here:

- `public/audio/english.mp3` — English voice sample
- `public/audio/hindi.mp3` — Hindi voice sample
- `public/audio/kannada.mp3` — Kannada voice sample
- `public/audio/telugu.mp3` — Telugu voice sample

When placed in this folder, the web player on `http://localhost:3000/#voices` will automatically play your audio files with interactive play/pause, animated waveforms, and seeking controls!
