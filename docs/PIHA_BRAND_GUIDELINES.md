# Piha AI — Brand & Visual Identity Guidelines

## Overview
Piha AI is named after the **Screaming Piha** (*Lipaugus vociferans*), renowned for having the loudest, clearest, and most resonant voice in nature (up to 116 dB). Our brand identity reflects extreme clarity, sub-second latency, and natural conversational cadence.

## Visual Mark
- **Icon Mark**: Screaming Piha bird silhouette vocalizing audio soundwaves (`/images/icons/Piha.webp`).
- **Aspect Ratio**: 3:2 transparent WebP.
- **Primary Color**: Deep Charcoal (`#111111`) and Warm Terracotta accents.

## Voice Persona Attributes
1. **Direct & Warm**: Conversational tone tailored for e-commerce growth & service qualification.
2. **Sub-second Latency**: Under 800ms end-to-end turn turnaround.
3. **Multilingual Code-Switching**: Fluent transition between English, Hindi, Telugu, and Tamil.
